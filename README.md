# Emberfall Clash

An original browser card duel. You face the Ashen Warden across a scorched felt
table: each round both sides commit 1-3 cards face-down, then reveal. Clash power
is the sum of ranks plus stacking combos — Pair (+4), Surge (+5), Chain (+6),
Resonance (+4) — plus Stone bulwark (+2 per Stone card) and a +3 bonus when your
majority element beats the opponent's in the Ember/Tide/Leaf cycle. The loser
takes damage equal to the power gap; first life total to reach zero loses.

Four elements are told apart by colour and shape (Ember triangle, Tide circle,
Leaf diamond, Stone square) so every card reads at a glance. The Warden cycles
through readable stances — Pressing, Guarding, Probing — and counters the element
you leaned on last round, so every hand is a decision about what to spend, what
to hold, and what to bait.

Play with mouse (click cards, press Clash) or keyboard (1-6 to pick, Enter to
clash, M to mute). Cards deal, snap and flip with CSS animation; every event has
a WebAudio sound. No network, no build step: open `game/index.html` in a desktop
browser.
